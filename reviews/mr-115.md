# MR !115 — Prevalence scoring floor

## Finding 1 — High — Score improves when an unrelated 300th file is added

`src/metrics/mod.rs:111` drops the absolute-count cap as soon as `total == 300`. With one unchanged finding, totals 299 and 300 score 75 and 90 respectively. With three findings they score 50 and 90. This directly contradicts the invariant documented at lines 86–88 that a score must never rise merely because the repository grew.

The added test currently locks in the defect instead of preventing it. Blend/extend the transition until the prevalence score is no more favorable than the prior state, or replace the hard switch with a monotonic rule.
